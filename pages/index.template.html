Leap Desktop

A graphical desktop for the Timex Sinclair TS2068, in Z80 assembly: overlapping windows, menus, a mouse, Calculator, Key Caps, Notepad, Paint and a sliding-tile puzzle, on a 56 KB DOCK cartridge. Emulator-qualified; not yet tried on hardware.
